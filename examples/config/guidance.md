How this budget uses its categories:
* Groceries: supermarkets, grocery stores, farmers markets, butchers, bulk and warehouse food shopping.
* Restaurants: sit-down and fast-food meals, takeout, food delivery apps, bars and pubs.
* Coffee: coffee shops, cafés, bakeries, tea and juice bars.
* Transport: public transit fares, rideshare and taxis, fuel, parking, tolls, bike and scooter rentals.
* Subscriptions: streaming services, software and apps, cloud storage, news sites, anything billed monthly or yearly online.
* Utilities: electricity, gas, water, trash, internet, mobile and home phone.
* Housing: rent, mortgage payments, property management companies, HOA dues, renters or home insurance.
* Entertainment: movie theaters, concerts and live music, museums, sports events, games, ticketing sites.
* Health: pharmacies, doctors, dentists, clinics, opticians, therapy, gyms and fitness classes.
* Shopping: clothing, electronics, home goods, furniture, hardware stores, gifts.
* Travel: flights, hotels and vacation rentals, airport shops, luggage, travel booking sites.
* Miscellaneous: cash withdrawals, fees, and anything that fits nowhere else.

When a merchant could fit two categories, pick the one that matches what it mostly sells.
